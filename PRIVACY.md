# QBT Camera — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 21 October 2026_

QBT Camera does not collect, store or send any personal data to QBT Studio or anyone else.

- **Camera and microphone.** Video and sound go directly from your iPhone to your own computer — over
  USB or your local Wi‑Fi network. Nothing is uploaded to the internet or any server.
- **Depth and face data.** LiDAR / TrueDepth depth, the person mask and face positions are computed
  on your devices for background effects and framing, used only for the current video frame, and never
  stored or transmitted anywhere except to your own computer.
- **Local network.** The iPhone app advertises itself on your local network (Bonjour) so your computer
  can find it. It does not connect to anything else.
- **No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.** The apps contain no analytics or advertising SDKs.
- **Settings.** Your preferences are stored only on your devices (on Windows in
  `%LOCALAPPDATA%\QBTCamera`).

Questions: contact QBT Studio.
